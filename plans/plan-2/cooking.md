<h2 id="rice" data-en="Rice Cooker">电饭煲</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🍚 干米120 mL（96 g）淘洗沥干。</li><li>加💧 水120 mL，放入电饭煲。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Rinse and drain 120 mL (96 g) 🍚 dry rice.</li><li>Add 120 mL 💧 water to the rice cooker.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>启动White Rice白米模式，约50分钟；均分到第2、5盘。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Use White Rice mode, about 50 minutes; divide equally between meals 2 and 5.</li></ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="oven" data-en="Oven">烤箱</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>烤箱设为400°F，启动预热。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Set the oven to 400°F and start preheating.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🥔 小土豆340 g洗净。</li><li>拌🫒 油½茶匙、🧂 盐⅛茶匙、黑胡椒⅛茶匙、🧄 蒜粉¼茶匙、🧅 洋葱粉¼茶匙。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Wash 340 g little 🥔 potatoes.</li><li>Toss with ½ tsp 🫒 oil, ⅛ tsp 🧂 salt, ⅛ tsp black pepper, ¼ tsp 🧄 garlic powder and ¼ tsp 🧅 onion powder.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🍗 鸡腿4块（465 g）擦干。</li><li>拌🫒 油½茶匙、🧂 盐⅛茶匙、🧄 蒜粉½茶匙、🧅 洋葱粉½茶匙、🌶️ 红椒粉1茶匙、🌿 牛至½茶匙。</li><li>前半周备料时，后半周鸡腿分装冷冻。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Pat dry 4 🍗 chicken thighs (465 g).</li><li>Coat with ½ tsp 🫒 oil, ⅛ tsp 🧂 salt, ½ tsp 🧄 garlic powder, ½ tsp 🧅 onion powder, 1 tsp 🌶️ paprika and ½ tsp 🌿 oregano.</li><li>During the first session, freeze the remaining thighs for the second half of the week.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🍖 半盒排骨300 g，淋包装内酱汁，裹锡纸。</li><li>前半周备料时，另半盒排骨冷冻。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Use half a box of 🍖 ribs (300 g including bones and sauce); add the packaged sauce and wrap in foil.</li><li>Freeze the other half during the first cooking session.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>确认烤箱达到400°F。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Confirm the oven has reached 400°F.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🥔 整颗土豆400°F烤40分钟至软；均分到第4、7盘。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Roast whole 🥔 potatoes at 400°F for 40 minutes until tender; divide equally between meals 4 and 7.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>400°F烤27分钟，中心达74°C。</li><li>第1盘2块掰碎；第3、6盘各1块。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Roast at 400°F for 27 minutes, to 74°C in the centre.</li><li>Shred 2 thighs for meal 1; place 1 thigh each in meals 3 and 6.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>400°F烤15–20分钟，中心达74°C；第4、7盘各¼盒。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Heat at 400°F for 15–20 minutes, to 74°C in the centre; put ¼ box each in meals 4 and 7.</li></ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="air-fryer" data-en="Air Fryer">空气炸锅</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🐟 三文鱼2块（320 g）擦干。</li><li>拌🫒 油¼茶匙、🧂 盐1/16茶匙、黑胡椒¼茶匙、🌶️ 红椒粉½茶匙。</li><li>前半周备料时，后半周三文鱼冷冻。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Pat dry 2 🐟 salmon portions (320 g).</li><li>Season with ¼ tsp 🫒 oil, 1/16 tsp 🧂 salt, ¼ tsp black pepper and ½ tsp 🌶️ paprika.</li><li>Freeze the second-half salmon during the first cooking session.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>空气炸锅设为400°F，启动预热。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Start preheating the air fryer to 400°F.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>约4分钟后，确认达到400°F。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>After about 4 minutes, confirm the air fryer has reached 400°F.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🐟 400°F烤8分钟至熟透；第4、7盘各1块。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Air-fry 🐟 salmon at 400°F for 8 minutes until cooked through; place 1 portion each in meals 4 and 7.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>取冷冻🐟 裹粉黑线鳕2片，不解冻、不加油。</li><li>400°F单层烤18–20分钟至熟透；第3、6盘各1片。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Use 2 frozen 🐟 breaded haddock fillets; do not thaw or add oil.</li><li>Air-fry in a single layer at 400°F for 18–20 minutes until cooked through; place 1 fillet each in meals 3 and 6.</li></ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="pot-one" data-en="Pot 1">煮锅一</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>煮锅一加💧 水烧开。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Bring 💧 water to a boil in pot 1.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>放入🥚 鸡蛋6个，煮约8分钟至蛋白蛋黄凝固，捞出冷却。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Add 6 🥚 eggs, boil for about 8 minutes until set, then remove and cool.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🥚 剥壳，第1、2、5盘各2个；第1盘的蛋掰碎。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Peel the 🥚 eggs; place 2 each in meals 1, 2 and 5. Break up the eggs for meal 1.</li></ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="pot-two" data-en="Pot 2">煮锅二</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>煮锅二加💧 水烧开。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Bring 💧 water to a boil in pot 2.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🍝 干意面100 g煮约8分钟后沥干。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Cook 100 g dry 🍝 spaghetti for about 8 minutes, then drain.</li></ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="small-wok" data-en="Saucepan">小锅</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🍄 蘑菇227 g，加🫒 油¼茶匙、黑胡椒¼茶匙、🧄 蒜粉½茶匙、🧅 洋葱粉½茶匙。</li><li>炒约7分钟；均分到第3、4、6、7盘。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Season 227 g 🍄 mushrooms with ¼ tsp 🫒 oil, ¼ tsp black pepper, ½ tsp 🧄 garlic powder and ½ tsp 🧅 onion powder.</li><li>Sauté for about 7 minutes; divide equally between meals 3, 4, 6 and 7.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🫒 油½茶匙，加黑胡椒¼茶匙、🧄 蒜粉½茶匙、🌿 意式香料½茶匙。</li><li>倒入🍅 Catelli番茄罗勒意面酱300 mL，煮2–3分钟；拌入🍝 意面再煮3分钟。</li><li>均分到第3、6盘。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Heat ½ tsp 🫒 oil with ¼ tsp black pepper, ½ tsp 🧄 garlic powder and ½ tsp 🌿 Italian seasoning.</li><li>Add 300 mL 🍅 Catelli Tomato &amp; Basil Pasta Sauce; simmer for 2–3 minutes, then toss with the 🍝 spaghetti for 3 minutes.</li><li>Divide equally between meals 3 and 6.</li></ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="large-wok" data-en="Wok">大锅</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🥬 菠菜284 g，加🫒 油1茶匙、🧄 蒜粉¼茶匙、🧅 洋葱粉¼茶匙。</li><li>炒约7分钟；均分到第4、7盘。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Sauté 284 g 🥬 spinach with 1 tsp 🫒 oil, ¼ tsp 🧄 garlic powder and ¼ tsp 🧅 onion powder for about 7 minutes.</li><li>Divide equally between meals 4 and 7.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🫛🥕 豌豆胡萝卜1罐沥干。</li><li>锅内加🫒 油½茶匙，中火炒🍗 鸡肉末454 g至中心74°C。</li><li>加入豌豆胡萝卜、🧄 蒜粉¼茶匙、🧅 洋葱粉¼茶匙、🥣 减盐酱油3茶匙、鸡粉⅛茶匙，炒热拌匀；均分到第2、5盘。</li><li>前半周备料时，后半周的生鸡肉末冷冻。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Drain 1 can 🫛🥕 peas and carrots.</li><li>Heat ½ tsp 🫒 oil and cook 454 g 🍗 ground chicken over medium heat to 74°C.</li><li>Add the vegetables, ¼ tsp 🧄 garlic powder, ¼ tsp 🧅 onion powder, 3 tsp 🥣 reduced-sodium soy sauce and ⅛ tsp chicken stock powder; heat through and divide equally between meals 2 and 5.</li><li>Freeze the second-half raw ground chicken during the first cooking session.</li></ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="finish" data-en="Finish">收尾</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>第1盘铺🌯 大号全麦卷饼皮1片，抹🥣 Chipotle蛋黄酱1茶匙（5 mL）。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Lay 1 large whole-wheat 🌯 tortilla in meal container 1; spread with 1 tsp (5 mL) 🥣 Chipotle mayonnaise.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>将掰碎的🍗 鸡腿和🥚 水煮蛋放入卷饼皮，卷好。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Add the shredded 🍗 thighs and broken-up 🥚 boiled eggs to the tortilla and roll up.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>餐盒入冰箱；🥚 水煮蛋随本批餐冷藏。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Refrigerate the meal containers and 🥚 boiled eggs for this batch.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🧼 清洗用过的厨具，擦净台面。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>🧼 Wash used cookware and wipe the counter.</li></ol>
</li>
</ol>
