<h2 id="rice" data-en="Rice Cooker">电饭煲</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🍚 干米120 mL（96 g）淘洗沥干。</li><li>加💧 水120 mL，放入电饭煲。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Rinse and drain 120 mL (96 g) 🍚 dry rice.</li><li>Add 120 mL 💧 water to the rice cooker.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>启动White Rice白米模式，完成启动后勾选。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Start White Rice mode and check this step once the cooker is running.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>约50分钟后，确认电饭煲完成煮饭，再开盖松散、分装🍚 米饭。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>After about 50 minutes, confirm the rice cycle is complete, then fluff and portion the 🍚 rice.</li>
</ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="oven" data-en="Oven">烤箱</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>烤箱设为400°F，启动预热。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Set the oven to 400°F and start preheating.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🥔 小土豆340 g洗净。</li><li>拌🫒 油½茶匙、🧂 盐⅛茶匙、黑胡椒⅛茶匙、🧄 蒜粉¼茶匙、🧅 洋葱粉¼茶匙。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Wash 340 g little 🥔 potatoes.</li><li>Toss with ½ tsp 🫒 oil, ⅛ tsp 🧂 salt, ⅛ tsp black pepper, ¼ tsp 🧄 garlic powder and ¼ tsp 🧅 onion powder.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🍗 鸡腿4块（465 g）擦干。</li><li>拌🫒 油½茶匙、🧂 盐⅛茶匙、🧄 蒜粉½茶匙、🧅 洋葱粉½茶匙、🌶️ 红椒粉1茶匙、🌿 牛至½茶匙。</li><li>前半周备料时，后半周鸡腿分装冷冻。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Pat dry 4 🍗 chicken thighs (465 g).</li><li>Coat with ½ tsp 🫒 oil, ⅛ tsp 🧂 salt, ½ tsp 🧄 garlic powder, ½ tsp 🧅 onion powder, 1 tsp 🌶️ paprika and ½ tsp 🌿 oregano.</li><li>During the first session, freeze the remaining thighs for the second half of the week.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🍖 半盒排骨300 g，淋包装内酱汁，裹锡纸。</li><li>前半周备料时，另半盒排骨冷冻。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Use half a box of 🍖 ribs (300 g including bones and sauce); add the packaged sauce and wrap in foil.</li><li>Freeze the other half during the first cooking session.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>确认烤箱达到400°F。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Confirm the oven has reached 400°F.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>将备好的土豆放入预热至400°F的烤箱，开始烹调后勾选。</li>
<li>约40分钟后检查熟度。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Load the prepared potatoes into the oven preheated to 400°F and check after starting.</li>
<li>Check for doneness after about 40 minutes.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>烤约20分钟时将🥔 土豆翻面，再放回继续烤。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>After about 20 minutes, turn the 🥔 potatoes and return them to the oven.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>确认熟度后取出：🥔 整颗土豆400°F烤40分钟至软；均分到第4、7盘。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Check doneness before removing: Roast whole 🥔 potatoes at 400°F for 40 minutes until tender; divide equally between meals 4 and 7.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>将备好的鸡大腿肉放入预热至400°F的烤箱，开始烹调后勾选。</li>
<li>约27分钟后检查熟度。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Load the prepared chicken thighs into the oven preheated to 400°F and check after starting.</li>
<li>Check for doneness after about 27 minutes.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>确认熟度后取出：400°F烤27分钟，中心达74°C。</li>
<li>第1盘2块掰碎；第3、6盘各1块。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Check doneness before removing: Roast at 400°F for 27 minutes, to 74°C in the centre.</li>
<li>Shred 2 thighs for meal 1; place 1 thigh each in meals 3 and 6.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>将备好的排骨放入预热至400°F的烤箱，开始烹调后勾选。</li>
<li>约20分钟后检查熟度。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Load the prepared ribs into the oven preheated to 400°F and check after starting.</li>
<li>Check for doneness after about 20 minutes.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>确认熟度后取出：400°F烤15–20分钟，中心达74°C；第4、7盘各¼盒。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Check doneness before removing: Heat at 400°F for 15–20 minutes, to 74°C in the centre; put ¼ box each in meals 4 and 7.</li>
</ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="air-fryer" data-en="Air Fryer">空气炸锅</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🐟 三文鱼4块（640 g）擦干。</li><li>拌🫒 油½茶匙、🧂 盐⅛茶匙、黑胡椒½茶匙、🌶️ 红椒粉1茶匙。</li><li>前半周备料时，后半周三文鱼冷冻。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Pat dry 4 🐟 salmon portions (640 g).</li><li>Season with ½ tsp 🫒 oil, ⅛ tsp 🧂 salt, ½ tsp black pepper and 1 tsp 🌶️ paprika.</li><li>Freeze the second-half salmon during the first cooking session.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>空气炸锅设为400°F，启动预热。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Start preheating the air fryer to 400°F.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>约4分钟后，确认达到400°F。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>After about 4 minutes, confirm the air fryer has reached 400°F.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>将备好的三文鱼放入预热至400°F的空气炸锅，开始烹调后勾选。</li>
<li>约8分钟后检查熟度。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Load the prepared salmon into the air fryer preheated to 400°F and check after starting.</li>
<li>Check for doneness after about 8 minutes.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>确认熟度后取出：🐟 400°F烤8分钟起检查至熟透；第3、4、6、7盘各1块。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Check doneness before removing: Air-fry 🐟 salmon at 400°F, checking from 8 minutes until cooked through; place 1 portion each in meals 3, 4, 6 and 7.</li>
</ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="pot-one" data-en="Pot 1">煮锅一</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>煮锅一加入💧 水，开火烧水；开火后即可勾选这一步。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Add 💧 water to Pot 1 and turn on the heat. Check this step once heating has started.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>看到💧 水持续沸腾后再勾选，随后放入食材；烧水不设固定时长。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Check only when the 💧 water reaches a steady boil, then add the food. No fixed boiling time.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>轻轻放入🥚 鸡蛋6个，开始煮蛋。</li>
<li>放入后勾选这一步；留意沸溢，等待时可穿插其他任务。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Gently add 6 🥚 eggs and start cooking.</li>
<li>Check after adding the food. Watch for boil-over while doing other tasks.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>约8分钟后，确认🥚 蛋白蛋黄凝固，捞出放💧 冷水冷却，保留锅内热水。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>After about 8 minutes, confirm the 🥚 eggs are set; lift into cold 💧 water to cool and keep the hot water in the pot.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🥚 剥壳，第1、2、5盘各2个；第1盘的蛋掰碎。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Peel the 🥚 eggs; place 2 each in meals 1, 2 and 5. Break up the eggs for meal 1.</li></ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="pot-two" data-en="Pot 2">煮锅二</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>煮锅二加入💧 水，开火烧水；开火后即可勾选这一步。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Add 💧 water to Pot 2 and turn on the heat. Check this step once heating has started.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>看到💧 水持续沸腾后再勾选，随后放入食材；烧水不设固定时长。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Check only when the 💧 water reaches a steady boil, then add the food. No fixed boiling time.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>放入🍝 干意面100 g，轻轻搅散。</li>
<li>放入后勾选这一步；留意沸溢，等待时可穿插其他任务。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Add 100 g dry 🍝 spaghetti and gently separate the strands.</li>
<li>Check after adding the food. Watch for boil-over while doing other tasks.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>约8分钟后按包装说明检查🍝 意面熟度，再取出沥干。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>After about 8 minutes, check the 🍝 spaghetti for doneness according to the package, then drain.</li>
</ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="small-wok" data-en="Saucepan">小锅</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🫒 油½茶匙，加黑胡椒¼茶匙、🧄 蒜粉½茶匙、🌿 意式香料½茶匙。</li><li>倒入🍅 Catelli番茄罗勒意面酱300 mL，煮2–3分钟；拌入🍝 意面再煮3分钟。</li><li>均分到第3、6盘。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Heat ½ tsp 🫒 oil with ¼ tsp black pepper, ½ tsp 🧄 garlic powder and ½ tsp 🌿 Italian seasoning.</li><li>Add 300 mL 🍅 Catelli Tomato &amp; Basil Pasta Sauce; simmer for 2–3 minutes, then toss with the 🍝 spaghetti for 3 minutes.</li><li>Divide equally between meals 3 and 6.</li></ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="large-wok" data-en="Wok">大锅</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🥬 菠菜1袋（284 g，每周2袋），加🫒 油1茶匙、🧄 蒜粉¼茶匙、🧅 洋葱粉¼茶匙。</li><li>炒约7分钟；均分到第4、7盘。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Sauté 1 bag (284 g; 2 bags per week) of 🥬 spinach with 1 tsp 🫒 oil, ¼ tsp 🧄 garlic powder and ¼ tsp 🧅 onion powder for about 7 minutes.</li><li>Divide equally between meals 4 and 7.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🫛🥕 豌豆胡萝卜1罐沥干。</li><li>锅内加🫒 油½茶匙，中火炒🍗 鸡肉末454 g至中心74°C。</li><li>加入豌豆胡萝卜、🧄 蒜粉¼茶匙、🧅 洋葱粉¼茶匙、🥣 减盐酱油3茶匙、鸡粉⅛茶匙，炒热拌匀；均分到第2、5盘。</li><li>前半周备料时，后半周的生鸡肉末冷冻。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Drain 1 can 🫛🥕 peas and carrots.</li><li>Heat ½ tsp 🫒 oil and cook 454 g 🍗 ground chicken over medium heat to 74°C.</li><li>Add the vegetables, ¼ tsp 🧄 garlic powder, ¼ tsp 🧅 onion powder, 3 tsp 🥣 reduced-sodium soy sauce and ⅛ tsp chicken stock powder; heat through and divide equally between meals 2 and 5.</li><li>Freeze the second-half raw ground chicken during the first cooking session.</li></ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="finish" data-en="Finish">收尾</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🫑 橙甜椒洗净擦干，取½个（约85 g）去籽、切细条；生切直接使用。剩余半个包好冷藏，留给下批卷饼。</li><li>第1盘铺🌯 大号全麦卷饼皮1片，抹🥣 Chipotle蛋黄酱1茶匙（5 mL）。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Wash and dry 🫑 orange bell pepper; deseed and thinly slice ½ pepper (about 85 g) to use raw. Wrap and refrigerate the remaining half for the next batch.</li><li>Lay 1 large whole-wheat 🌯 tortilla in meal container 1; spread with 1 tsp (5 mL) 🥣 Chipotle mayonnaise.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>将掰碎的🍗 鸡腿、🥚 水煮蛋和🫑 生橙甜椒条放入卷饼皮，卷好。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Add the shredded 🍗 thighs, broken-up 🥚 boiled eggs and 🫑 raw orange bell pepper strips to the tortilla and roll up.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>餐盒入冰箱；🥚 水煮蛋随本批餐冷藏。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Refrigerate the meal containers and 🥚 boiled eggs for this batch.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🧼 所有餐盒入冰箱后，最后清洗用过的厨具，擦净台面。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>🧼 After refrigerating all meals, finish by washing used cookware and wiping the counter.</li></ol>
</li>
</ol>
