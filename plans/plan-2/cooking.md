<h2 id="rice" data-en="Rice Cooker">电饭煲</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🍚 干米120 g（约160 mL；首次称重核对刻度）淘洗沥干。</li><li>加💧 水约160 mL，放入电饭煲。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Rinse and drain 120 g (about 160 mL; calibrate the cup by weighing once) 🍚 dry rice.</li><li>Add about 160 mL 💧 water to the rice cooker.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>启动White Rice白米模式，完成启动后勾选。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Start White Rice mode and check this step once the cooker is running.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>约50分钟后，确认电饭煲完成煮饭，再开盖松散，直接均分到第1、5盘，每份对应生米60 g；留在餐盘中待浇咖喱，不放入炒锅。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>After about 50 minutes, confirm the rice cycle is complete, then fluff and divide the 🍚 rice directly between meals 1 and 5 (60 g dry rice each). Leave it in the containers for the curry topping; do not transfer it to the frying pan.</li>
</ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="oven" data-en="Oven">烤箱</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>烤箱设为400°F，启动预热。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Set the oven to 400°F and start preheating.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🥔 The Little Potato Company小土豆1袋（680 g）洗净，切成大小相近的薯角；烤好后均分两份，每份对应生重340 g。</li><li>拌🫒 油½茶匙、🧂 盐⅛茶匙、黑胡椒⅛茶匙、🧄 蒜粉¼茶匙、🧅 洋葱粉¼茶匙。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Wash one 680 g bag of The Little Potato Company 🥔 little potatoes and cut into evenly sized wedges; divide the cooked batch equally; each portion corresponds to 340 g raw potatoes.</li><li>Toss with ½ tsp 🫒 oil, ⅛ tsp 🧂 salt, ⅛ tsp black pepper, ¼ tsp 🧄 garlic powder and ¼ tsp 🧅 onion powder.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🍗 鸡腿4块（465 g）擦干。</li><li>拌🫒 油½茶匙、🧂 盐⅛茶匙、🧄 蒜粉½茶匙、🧅 洋葱粉½茶匙、🌶️ 红椒粉1茶匙、🌿 牛至½茶匙。</li><li>前半周备料时，后半周鸡腿分装冷冻。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Pat dry 4 🍗 chicken thighs (465 g).</li><li>Coat with ½ tsp 🫒 oil, ⅛ tsp 🧂 salt, ½ tsp 🧄 garlic powder, ½ tsp 🧅 onion powder, 1 tsp 🌶️ paprika and ½ tsp 🌿 oregano.</li><li>During the first session, freeze the remaining thighs for the second half of the week.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🍖 半盒排骨300 g，淋包装内酱汁，裹锡纸。</li><li>前半周备料时，另半盒排骨冷冻。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Use half a box of 🍖 ribs (300 g including bones and sauce); add the packaged sauce and wrap in foil.</li><li>Freeze the other half during the first cooking session.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>确认烤箱达到400°F。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Confirm the oven has reached 400°F.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>将备好的土豆放入预热至400°F的烤箱，开始烹调后勾选。</li>
<li>约40分钟后检查熟度。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Load the prepared potatoes into the oven preheated to 400°F and check after starting.</li>
<li>Check for doneness after about 40 minutes.</li>
</ol>
</li>

<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>确认熟度后取出：🥔 整颗土豆400°F烤40分钟至软；均分到第3、7盘。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Check doneness before removing: Roast whole 🥔 potatoes at 400°F for 40 minutes until tender; divide equally between meals 3 and 7.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>将备好的鸡大腿肉放入预热至400°F的烤箱，开始烹调后勾选。</li>
<li>约27分钟后检查熟度。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Load the prepared chicken thighs into the oven preheated to 400°F and check after starting.</li>
<li>Check for doneness after about 27 minutes.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>确认熟度后取出：400°F烤27分钟，中心达74°C。</li>
<li>第4盘2块掰碎；第2、6盘各1块。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Check doneness before removing: Roast at 400°F for 27 minutes, to 74°C in the centre.</li>
<li>Shred 2 thighs for meal 4; place 1 thigh each in meals 2 and 6.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>将备好的排骨放入预热至400°F的烤箱，开始烹调后勾选。</li>
<li>约20分钟后检查熟度。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Load the prepared ribs into the oven preheated to 400°F and check after starting.</li>
<li>Check for doneness after about 20 minutes.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>确认熟度后取出：400°F烤15–20分钟，中心达74°C；第3、7盘各¼盒。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Check doneness before removing: Heat at 400°F for 15–20 minutes, to 74°C in the centre; put ¼ box each in meals 3 and 7.</li>
</ol>
</li>


</ol>
<h2 id="air-fryer" data-en="Air Fryer">空气炸锅</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🐟 三文鱼2块（320 g）和罗非鱼2块（约350 g）擦干。</li><li>拌🫒 油½茶匙、🧂 盐⅛茶匙、黑胡椒½茶匙、🌶️ 红椒粉1茶匙。</li><li>前半周备料时，后半周三文鱼和罗非鱼冷冻。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Pat dry 2 🐟 salmon portions (320 g) and 2 tilapia fillets (about 350 g).</li><li>Season with ½ tsp 🫒 oil, ⅛ tsp 🧂 salt, ½ tsp black pepper and 1 tsp 🌶️ paprika.</li><li>Freeze the second-half salmon and tilapia during the first cooking session.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>空气炸锅设为400°F，启动预热。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Start preheating the air fryer to 400°F.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>约4分钟后，确认达到400°F。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>After about 4 minutes, confirm the air fryer has reached 400°F.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>将备好的三文鱼和罗非鱼单层放入预热至400°F的空气炸锅，开始烹调后勾选。</li>
<li>约8分钟后检查熟度。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Load the prepared salmon and tilapia in a single layer into the air fryer preheated to 400°F and check after starting.</li>
<li>Check for doneness after about 8 minutes.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>确认熟度后取出：🐟 400°F烤8分钟起检查至熟透；三文鱼放第2、6盘，罗非鱼放第3、7盘，各1块；炸篮装不下时分批。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Check doneness before removing: Air-fry 🐟 salmon and tilapia at 400°F, checking from 8 minutes until cooked through; place salmon in meals 2 and 6 and tilapia in meals 3 and 7, one fillet each. Cook in batches if the basket is too small.</li>
</ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="pot-one" data-en="Pot 1">煮锅一</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>煮锅一加入💧 水，开火烧水；开火后即可勾选这一步。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Add 💧 water to Pot 1 and turn on the heat. Check this step once heating has started.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>看到💧 水持续沸腾后再勾选，随后放入食材；烧水不设固定时长。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Check only when the 💧 water reaches a steady boil, then add the food. No fixed boiling time.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>轻轻放入🥚 鸡蛋6个，开始煮蛋。</li>
<li>放入后勾选这一步；留意沸溢，等待时可穿插其他任务。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>Gently add 6 🥚 eggs and start cooking.</li>
<li>Check after adding the food. Watch for boil-over while doing other tasks.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol lang="zh-CN" class="cooking-source-zh">
<li>约8分钟后，确认🥚 蛋白蛋黄凝固，捞出放💧 冷水冷却，保留锅内热水。</li>
</ol>
<ol lang="en" class="cooking-source-en" hidden>
<li>After about 8 minutes, confirm the 🥚 eggs are set; lift into cold 💧 water to cool and keep the hot water in the pot.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🥚 剥壳，第1、4、5盘各2个；第4盘的蛋掰碎。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Peel the 🥚 eggs; place 2 each in meals 1, 4 and 5. Break up the eggs for meal 4.</li></ol>
</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="small-wok" data-en="Saucepan">小锅</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🫒 油½茶匙，加黑胡椒¼茶匙、🧄 蒜粉½茶匙、🌿 意式香料½茶匙。</li><li>倒入🍅 Catelli番茄罗勒意面酱300 mL，煮2–3分钟；拌入🍝 意面再煮3分钟。</li><li>均分到第2、6盘。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Heat ½ tsp 🫒 oil with ¼ tsp black pepper, ½ tsp 🧄 garlic powder and ½ tsp 🌿 Italian seasoning.</li><li>Add 300 mL 🍅 Catelli Tomato &amp; Basil Pasta Sauce; simmer for 2–3 minutes, then toss with the 🍝 spaghetti for 3 minutes.</li><li>Divide equally between meals 2 and 6.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>💧 小锅加入约300 mL水，开火加热；启动后可做其他任务。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Add about 300 mL 💧 water to the saucepan and start heating; other tasks can be done while waiting.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>💧 确认小锅里的水已经烧开，再进行下一步。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Confirm the 💧 water in the saucepan has come to a boil before continuing.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🍛 90 g装咖喱切下⅖包（36 g），切碎。</li><li>小锅暂时关火，将咖喱块直接加入热水，搅拌至溶解。</li><li>重新开小火煮约5分钟，期间搅拌；不另外加油。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Chop ⅖ of the 90 g 🍛 curry pack (36 g).</li><li>Turn off the saucepan heat, add the roux directly to the hot water and stir until dissolved.</li><li>Return to low heat and simmer for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, without additional oil.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🍛 确认咖喱块完全溶解、酱汁热透；过稠时少量补💧 水，关火。</li><li>咖喱汁均分到第1、5盘，直接浇在🍚 米饭和🍗 鸡肉蔬菜上；其他食材未完成时可先分装酱汁。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Check that the 🍛 roux has dissolved and the sauce is hot; add a little 💧 water if too thick, then turn off the heat.</li><li>Divide the sauce equally between meals 1 and 5 and pour directly over the 🍚 rice and 🍗 chicken and vegetables. If other ingredients are not ready, portion the sauce first.</li></ol>
</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="large-wok" data-en="Wok">大锅</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🥬 菠菜1袋（284 g，每周2袋），加🫒 油1茶匙、🧄 蒜粉¼茶匙、🧅 洋葱粉¼茶匙。</li><li>炒约7分钟；均分到第3、7盘。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Sauté 1 bag (284 g; 2 bags per week) of 🥬 spinach with 1 tsp 🫒 oil, ¼ tsp 🧄 garlic powder and ¼ tsp 🧅 onion powder for about 7 minutes.</li><li>Divide equally between meals 3 and 7.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🫛🥕 豌豆胡萝卜1罐沥干。</li><li>锅内加🫒 油½茶匙，中火炒🍗 鸡肉末半包（227 g）至中心74°C。</li><li>加入豌豆胡萝卜、🧄 蒜粉¼茶匙、🧅 洋葱粉¼茶匙，炒热拌匀，均分到第1、5盘。</li><li>前半周备料时，剩下半包（227 g）生鸡肉末冷冻，留待后半周使用。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Drain 1 can 🫛🥕 peas and carrots.</li><li>Heat ½ tsp 🫒 oil and cook half a pack (227 g) of 🍗 ground chicken over medium heat to 74°C.</li><li>Add the vegetables, ¼ tsp 🧄 garlic powder and ¼ tsp 🧅 onion powder; heat through, then divide equally between meals 1 and 5.</li><li>During the first session, freeze the remaining half pack (227 g) of raw ground chicken for the second half of the week.</li></ol>
</li>


</ol>
<h2 id="finish" data-en="Finish">收尾</h2>
<ol>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🧆 取冷冻熟牛肉丸6颗，单层放入可微波容器，盖可微波盖并留排气口；剩余牛肉丸密封冷冻。</li><li>放入微波炉，加热1分半；启动后可做其他任务。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Place 6 frozen fully cooked 🧆 meatballs in a single layer in a microwave-safe container; cover with a vented microwave-safe lid and keep the remainder frozen.</li><li>Microwave for 1½ minutes; other tasks can be done after starting.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🧆 1分半后取出，注意蒸汽；确认中心热透，未热透则继续短时加热并检查。</li><li>第1、5盘各放3颗，搭配咖喱米饭。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>After 1½ minutes, remove carefully and watch for steam. Check that the 🧆 centres are thoroughly hot; if needed, continue in short intervals and check again.</li><li>Place 3 each in meals 1 and 5 with the curry rice.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🥦 取冷冻西兰花125 g（500 g袋的¼），不用提前解冻，倒入微波炉适用的碗；剩余密封继续冷冻。</li><li>加入💧 水1汤匙（15 mL，＝3茶匙），盖上可微波盖并留排气口；高火先微波2分钟。不连普通包装袋加热；包装有明确水量和时间时以包装为准。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>Place 125 g frozen 🥦 broccoli (¼ of a 500 g bag) in a microwave-safe bowl without thawing; reseal and freeze the remainder.</li><li>Add 1 tbsp 💧 water (15 mL, or 3 tsp), cover with a vented microwave-safe lid and microwave on high for 2 minutes initially. Do not microwave the ordinary packaging bag; use package water and timing instructions when specified.</li></ol>
</li>
<li class="cooking-task-source">
<ol class="cooking-source-zh" lang="zh"><li>🥦 2分钟后小心蒸汽，翻动并检查中心是否热透；不够时每次追加30秒，直到热透。通常总共约2–3分钟，按微波炉功率和实际熟度调整。</li><li>沥掉多余💧 水，均分到第3、7盘，每份对应冷冻重量62.5 g；不额外加油或盐。</li></ol>
<ol class="cooking-source-en" lang="en" hidden><li>After 2 minutes, open carefully to avoid steam, stir and check that the centre of the 🥦 broccoli is hot. If needed, heat in additional 30-second intervals until hot throughout. Usually allow about 2–3 minutes total, adjusting for microwave power and doneness.</li><li>Drain excess 💧 water and divide equally between meals 3 and 7 (62.5 g frozen weight each), without additional oil or salt.</li></ol>
